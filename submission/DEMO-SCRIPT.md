## Recording notes

- Target 2:45 and keep the final video below three minutes. Include readable functioning-app footage.
- Keep the buyer login ready outside the recording. Hide passwords, API keys, email addresses and unrelated browser tabs. Do not show .env.local.
- If shortening waiting time, mark the cut honestly. Never describe an unverified or synthetic result as an actual PayPal authorization.
- If network delays exceed the target, retry the rehearsal or clearly label previously recorded footage from the same demonstrated flow. Do not imply a different case or amount is the current result.
- Keep the limit visible: USD up to 500, configured PayPal sandbox merchant only; no capture, bank transfer or identity verification.
- Upload the final demo to publicly visible YouTube and put its link in the submission after review.
